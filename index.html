About

Two decades of building, one symposium of purpose

The Department of Mechanical Engineering

Established in 2009, the Department of Mechanical Engineering at GCE Bargur has offered a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering since 2009–2010, backed by well-equipped, state-of-the-art laboratories. Core study areas span fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, heat transfer, solid mechanics, materials engineering, manufacturing, energy systems, dynamics & control systems, CAD and CIM.

Faculty research spans micro machining, IC engines, alternative fuels, nano coatings, composite materials, surface engineering, corrosion, optimisation, nano materials, and refrigeration & air conditioning — alongside regular training programmes, workshops and seminars for staff and students.

PHANTASM'27 — Vaagai 2k26

A national-level technical symposium organised by the Department of Mechanical Engineering, designed to bring innovation, engineering excellence and creative problem-solving onto one platform. Rooted in Mechanical Engineering, it is a convergence point for students, academicians and industry enthusiasts to take on real-world engineering challenges through technical events, workshops, design challenges and hands-on competitions.

"To inspire a generation of engineers who innovate responsibly, think critically, and lead confidently in shaping the future of technology and industry."

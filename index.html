About the symposium

Where engineering meets ambition.

Department of Mechanical Engineering

Established in 2009, the department offers B.E. Mechanical Engineering with state-of-the-art laboratories and broad study across fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, heat transfer, solid mechanics, materials, manufacturing, energy systems, dynamics & control, CAD and CIM.

Faculty research includes micro machining, IC engines, alternative fuels, nano coatings, composites, surface engineering, corrosion, optimisation, nano materials, and refrigeration and air conditioning.

Vaagai 2k26

A national-level technical symposium designed to bring students, academicians and industry enthusiasts together to explore real-world engineering challenges through technical events, workshops, design challenges and hands-on competitions.

Our vision: inspire engineers who innovate responsibly, think critically and lead confidently in shaping the future of technology and industry.

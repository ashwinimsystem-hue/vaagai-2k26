Technical Events

01 · Technical

Paper Presentation

17 Sep 20263rd Year Classroom₹200 solo / ₹400 teamMax 4

Present work related to engineering, technology or innovation.

  • 10 min presentation + 5 min Q&A.
  • 8–10 slides, excluding title and thank-you slides.
  • Original work only; plagiarism leads to disqualification.
  • IEEE format is recommended; all team members should contribute.
  • College ID is required.

Judging & prizes

Originality, technical depth, relevance, clarity, visuals, delivery and Q&A. ₹2,000 / ₹1,500 / ₹1,000.

02 · Technical

CAD Modeling

17 Sep 2026CAD Lab₹200Individual90 min

Model a mechanical component/drawing supplied at the event.

  • CATIA and SOLIDWORKS; other software requires prior approval.
  • No electronic gadgets.
  • Unfair assistance or malpractice causes disqualification.
  • Unfinished work is evaluated as-is at the time limit.

Judging & prizes

Mass properties, dimensional accuracy, geometry, feature accuracy and completion. ₹1,500 / ₹1,000 / ₹500.

03 · Technical

ANSYS Simulation Challenge

17 Sep 2026CAD Lab₹200Individual

Use ANSYS Workbench / Fluent provided at the venue to solve an on-the-spot problem.

  • Prepared files are not allowed.
  • Independent work only.
  • Complete within the allotted time.
  • No unauthorised assistance.

Judging & prizes

Geometry, materials, mesh, boundary conditions, technical accuracy, result interpretation, problem-solving and time management. ₹1,500 / ₹1,000 / ₹500.

04 · Technical

Glider Competition

18 Sep 2026Indoor Stadium₹200 single / ₹250 doublesMax 2

Build a stable, student-made glider and compete for maximum valid horizontal distance.

  • Allowed: wood, foam board/depron, cardboard and thermocol; other materials need approval.
  • Wingspan ≤600 mm; length ≤450 mm; weight ≤200 g.
  • Prefabricated gliders only; on-spot fabrication prohibited.
  • Stall, dive, loop or crash results in disqualification.

Judging & prizes

Stability, aerodynamic efficiency, structural strength, CG placement, safety, material efficiency, optimisation and technical explanation. ₹1,500 / ₹1,000 / ₹500.

05 · Technical

Technical Quiz

18 Sep 2026Smart Class₹100Max 3/team

Multiple physical MCQ rounds covering mechanical engineering, technical trivia and current trends.

  • Electronic gadgets are prohibited.
  • Accuracy, speed and consistency determine performance.

Prizes

₹1,000 / ₹750 / ₹500.

06 · Technical

Line Follower

18 Sep 20263rd Year Classroom₹3002–3/team

Build an autonomous robot for a black-line-on-white track containing curves, zig-zags and 90° turns.

  • Robot ≤20×20×20 cm and ≤2 kg.
  • Onboard 12–15 V only.
  • No remote, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi control.
  • Ready-made kits prohibited.
  • Maximum 2 min calibration and up to 3 restarts.
  • Touching the robot adds 5 seconds; stopwatch continues through restarts.
  • Arena damage causes disqualification.

Judging & prizes

Completion time, accuracy and penalties. ₹2,000 / ₹1,500 / ₹1,000.

# Vaagai 2k26 — Website Plan

## Information architecture

- Home — event identity, dates, college/department, primary registration CTA
- About — Vaagai 2k26, Department of Mechanical Engineering, college information, vision
- Events — Technical / Non-Technical categories with event detail views
- Schedule — Day 1 (17 Sep 2026) / Day 2 (18 Sep 2026)
- Registration — embedded Google Form
- Rules & Guidelines — general rules plus category/event-specific rules
- Coordinators — faculty and student coordinators
- Venue & Contact — GCE Bargur, contact/social links
- Poster — official event poster

## UX flow

Home → Events → Event Details → Register
Home → Schedule
Home → Rules & Guidelines
Home → About / Coordinators / Venue & Contact

## Design constraint

Use the information architecture and user-flow approach as inspiration only. Do not copy Synerix26.site's visual theme, colors, typography, illustrations, or branding. Vaagai should retain its own Tamil-culture / temple-inspired visual identity.

## Content strategy

Event rules, fees, team sizes, prizes, venues, and schedules must be easy to update because these details may change before the symposium. Avoid treating current event details as permanently final.

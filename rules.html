Line Follower

Teams build an autonomous robot that follows a black-line track, competing for the fastest completion time.

18 Sep 20263rd Year Classroom₹300 registration2–3 members
  • Inter-department and inter-college teams are allowed.
  • Robot limit: 20×20×20 cm and ≤2 kg.
  • Onboard power only (12–15V).
  • Strictly autonomous; remote, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi control are prohibited.
  • Maximum 2 minutes calibration and up to 3 restarts.
  • Touching the robot during a run adds a 5-second penalty.
  • Ready-made kits such as Lego Mindstorms are prohibited.
  • Damage to the arena leads to disqualification.

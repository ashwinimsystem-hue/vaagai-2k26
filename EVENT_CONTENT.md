Source: organiser-provided **New Rules and Regulation 2026.pdf**. Values below are the current approved working details; unspecified items remain to be announced.

## Technical Events

### 1. Paper Presentation
- Date: 17 September 2026
- Time: To be announced
- Venue: 3rd Year Classroom
- Registration fee: ₹200 solo / ₹400 team
- Participation: Individual or team
- Team size: Maximum 4
- Topic/theme: Engineering, technology or innovation
- Submission deadline: To be announced
- Presentation duration: 10 min presentation + 5 min Q&A
- Slides: 8–10 excluding title and thank-you slides
- Rules: Original work only; plagiarism leads to disqualification; IEEE format recommended; papers must be submitted by deadline; all team members should contribute; college ID required.
- Judging criteria: Originality, technical depth, relevance, clarity, visuals, delivery, Q&A performance
- Prizes: ₹2000 / ₹1500 / ₹1000
- Coordinator: To be announced

### 2. CAD Modeling
- Date: 17 September 2026
- Time: To be announced
- Venue: CAD Lab
- Registration fee: ₹200
- Participation: Individual
- Software: CATIA and SOLIDWORKS; other software requires prior coordinator approval
- Problem format: Mechanical component/drawing provided at the event
- Duration: 90 minutes
- Rules: Model the given component within the allotted time; no electronic gadgets; malpractice/unfair assistance causes disqualification; unfinished model at time limit is evaluated as-is.
- Judging criteria: Mass properties, dimensional accuracy, model geometry, feature accuracy, model completion
- Prizes: ₹1500 / ₹1000 / ₹500
- Coordinator: To be announced

### 3. ANSYS Simulation Challenge
- Date: 17 September 2026
- Time: To be announced
- Venue: CAD Lab
- Registration fee: ₹200
- Participation: Individual
- Software/module: ANSYS Workbench / Fluent provided at venue
- Problem format: Problem statement given on the spot
- Duration: To be announced
- Rules: Prepared files not allowed; must complete within allotted time; independent work only; no unauthorised assistance.
- Judging criteria: Geometry, material properties, mesh, boundary conditions, technical accuracy, result interpretation, problem-solving and time management
- Prizes: ₹1500 / ₹1000 / ₹500
- Coordinator: To be announced

### 4. Glider Competition
- Date: 18 September 2026
- Time: To be announced
- Venue: Indoor Stadium
- Registration fee: ₹200 single / ₹250 doubles
- Participation: Individual or team
- Team size: Up to 2
- Materials allowed: Wood, foam board/depron, cardboard, thermocol; other materials require coordinator approval
- Mandatory limits: Wingspan ≤600 mm; length ≤450 mm; weight ≤200 g
- Performance criteria: Maximum valid horizontal distance; only stable controlled flights count. Stall, dive, loop or crash = disqualification.
- Rules: Open to engineering students; prefabricated gliders only; on-spot fabrication prohibited.
- Judging criteria: Stability, aerodynamic efficiency, structural strength, CG placement, safety, material efficiency, structural optimisation and technical explanation
- Prizes: ₹1500 / ₹1000 / ₹500
- Coordinator: To be announced

### 5. Technical Quiz
- Date: 18 September 2026
- Time: To be announced
- Venue: Smart Class
- Registration fee: ₹100
- Participation: Team
- Team size: Maximum 3
- Rounds: Multiple physical MCQ rounds
- Rules: Questions cover mechanical engineering, technical trivia and current trends; electronic gadgets prohibited.
- Scoring: Correct answers, with speed and consistency considered
- Judging criteria: Accuracy, speed and consistency across rounds
- Prizes: ₹1000 / ₹750 / ₹500
- Coordinator: To be announced

### 6. Line Follower
- Date: 18 September 2026
- Time: To be announced
- Venue: 3rd Year Classroom
- Registration fee: ₹300
- Participation: Team
- Team size: 2–3; inter-department/inter-college allowed
- Robot limits: ≤20×20×20 cm and ≤2 kg
- Power limits: Onboard 12–15 V only
- Track/run format: Black line on white background with curves, zig-zags and 90° turns
- Rules: Fully autonomous; no remote/Bluetooth/Wi-Fi control; ready-made kits prohibited; max 2 min calibration; up to 3 restarts; arena damage causes disqualification.
- Penalties: Touching robot adds +5 seconds; stopwatch continues through restarts.
- Judging criteria: Completion time, accuracy and penalties
- Prizes: ₹2000 / ₹1500 / ₹1000
- Coordinator: To be announced
